# SOL 200 — The Story

Four astronauts who know each other from Earth face crises on Mars while a shared secret from their past surfaces.

**The central question: does Damon deserve to lead?**

Each chapter puts the player in one character's shoes, gives them one hard decision with two options (A or B), and teaches one lesson about people, not only about Mars.

### Chapter 1: The Commander — *playable prototype*
Play Damon, who fears he doesn't deserve to lead. In a Sol 200 dust storm he chooses: **go out to clean the panels (A)** or **stay in and share power (B)**.
> **Lesson:** leaders listen, and asking for help is not weakness.

### Chapter 2: Damon's Past — *planned*
Play Damon. A childhood flashback shows a lucky shortcut, unearned credit, and loss. Then Earth asks who decided: **claim the credit (A)** or **share it with Marcus (B)**.
> **Lesson:** shortcuts have a cost.

### Chapter 3: Marcus — *planned*
Play Marcus, who fears being ignored again. During a communications blackout he decides: **send his full honest report (A)** or **hide the painful parts (B)**.
> **Lesson:** silence isn't innocent.

### Chapter 4: Diego — *planned*
Play Diego, who avoids confrontation. A flashback of feeling like an outsider shows how humor became his shield. He hides a water leak: **admit it and repair fully (A)** or **keep the quick patch (B)**.
> **Lesson:** half-solutions only delay problems.

### Chapter 5: Layla — *planned*
Play Layla, who fears loss. Her flashback and her plant Hope reveal what she lost. She must **save Hope (A)** or **protect the crew's water (B)**, and she finally breaks her silence.
> **Lesson:** honesty can be kind.

### Chapter 6: The Sun — *planned*
All four play together, building a radiation shelter during a solar flare as the full truth comes out.
> **Lesson:** teamwork decides what survives.

## Threads across the series

- **Earth's question:** "Who made the decisions?" It starts at the end of Chapter 1 and follows the crew through every chapter.
- **The secret:** a past training accident that each chapter reveals from a different character's side.
- **The Team meter:** trust built or lost in every chapter decides the ending.

## Endings

- **Team trust 50 or more:** the crew opens up and sends one honest report to Earth together.
- **Team trust below 50:** accusations fly and they part ways, never speaking again.

## The Sun, from start to finish

Radiation from a solar flare is Chapter 6's climax. Chapter 1 already foreshadows it: the game reads **live NASA DONKI solar-flare data**, and today's real Sun decides how risky it is to step outside.

## The crew

- **Damon Carter** — Commander (playable in Chapter 1). Fears he does not deserve to lead.
- **Marcus Bell** — Deputy commander. Fears being ignored again.
- **Diego Ramirez** — Mechanical engineer. Fears conflict, so he hides behind jokes.
- **Layla Haddad** — Plant scientist. Fears losing what she loves.
